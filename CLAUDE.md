# CLAUDE.md

## Writing style

- All page text should be written casually, as if written by me (the site owner) in first person. Think a player sharing their findings, not a technical manual or marketing copy.
- Never use em dashes. Rewrite the sentence, or use a comma, colon, or parentheses instead.

## Accessibility and SEO

- The page should have good a11y: semantic HTML, labeled form controls, aria attributes where needed (live regions, labelled sections), visible focus states, WCAG AA contrast in light and dark mode, and never rely on color alone to convey meaning.
- The page should have good SEO tags: title, meta description, canonical URL, Open Graph and Twitter cards, and JSON-LD structured data. Keep them in sync with the page content when it changes.
